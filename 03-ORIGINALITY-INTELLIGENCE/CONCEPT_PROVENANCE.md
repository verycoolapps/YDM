# CONCEPT PROVENANCE
## YOUR DEAD MEAT — Conceptual Development & Creative Origins Documentation

---

**Purpose:** Documents conceptual development, creative origins, and the evolution of an original idea.  
**Scope:** All projects. Concept provenance is especially critical for high-value creative IP.  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Dependencies:** `ORIGINALITY_ENGINE.md`, `REFERENCE_SOURCE_TRACKER.md`  

---

## CONCEPT PROVENANCE PHILOSOPHY

The strongest IP position is one where the creator can trace their idea from initial concept through to final deliverable. A provenance chain showing:

1. Initial concept note / sketch / brief
2. First iteration or prototype
3. Development milestones
4. Final deliverable

...is far more credible than a single final file with a claimed creation date.

---

## CONCEPT PROVENANCE RECORD

```yaml
# CONCEPT PROVENANCE RECORD
# Project ID: YDM-[ID]

concept_provenance:
  
  origin_story:
    concept_origin_date: "[DATE — approximate if exact unknown]"
    origin_description: "[HOW DID THIS IDEA COME TO EXIST? In creator's own words]"
    initial_format: "Mental concept | Written note | Sketch | Verbal brief | Email | Meeting notes"
    origin_evidence: "[WHAT EVIDENCE OF THIS ORIGIN EXISTS?]"
    
  development_milestones:
    - milestone: "Initial concept"
      date: "[DATE]"
      description: "[WHAT EXISTED AT THIS POINT]"
      evidence: "[WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS — file, email, note, etc.]"
      
    - milestone: "First prototype/draft"
      date: "[DATE]"
      description: "[WHAT WAS CREATED]"
      evidence: "[EVIDENCE]"
      
    - milestone: "Major revision [describe]"
      date: "[DATE]"
      description: "[WHAT CHANGED AND WHY]"
      evidence: "[EVIDENCE]"
      
    - milestone: "Final deliverable"
      date: "[DATE]"
      description: "[FINAL STATE]"
      evidence: "[EVIDENCE]"
      
  creative_evolution:
    initial_concept_vs_final: >
      [DESCRIBE HOW THE WORK EVOLVED FROM INITIAL CONCEPT TO FINAL FORM —
      this narrative demonstrates genuine creative process]
    abandoned_directions:
      - "[CREATIVE DIRECTION EXPLORED BUT ABANDONED — and why]"
    pivotal_decisions:
      - "[DECISION THAT SIGNIFICANTLY SHAPED THE FINAL WORK]"
        
  influence_acknowledgment:
    genuine_influences:
      - "[WORK OR PERSON THAT GENUINELY INFLUENCED THIS PROJECT]"
        type: "Visual influence | Conceptual influence | Technical influence"
        how_different: "[HOW THIS WORK DIFFERS FROM THAT INFLUENCE]"
        
  provenance_strength: "Strong (documented from day 1) | Moderate | Weak (retroactive)"
  provenance_gaps:
    - "[ANY PERIOD IN THE PROJECT WHERE DOCUMENTATION IS MISSING]"
```

## PROVENANCE NARRATIVE TEMPLATE

For inclusion in formal reports, use this narrative format:

```
CONCEPT PROVENANCE NARRATIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[PROJECT TITLE] originated on approximately [DATE] when [CREATOR NAME]
[DESCRIBE HOW THE IDEA CAME TO EXIST].

The initial concept was documented as [FORMAT OF INITIAL DOCUMENTATION].
Evidence of this origin includes: [WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS].

The project developed through the following stages:
  [DATE]: [STAGE DESCRIPTION]
  [DATE]: [STAGE DESCRIPTION]
  
The final work differs from the initial concept in that [KEY EVOLUTION].
These changes reflect [CREATIVE RATIONALE].

This provenance record is based on [USER-DECLARED INFORMATION / DOCUMENTED EVIDENCE].
```
