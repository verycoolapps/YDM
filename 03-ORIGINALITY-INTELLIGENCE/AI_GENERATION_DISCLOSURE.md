## THE AI DISCLOSURE IMPERATIVE

Transparency about AI assistance is both ethically correct and strategically important for IP protection:

1. **Legal clarity:** Copyright ownership of purely AI-generated content is legally uncertain in most jurisdictions as of 2024. Human creative direction, selection, and modification are what establish protectable rights.
2. **Credibility:** A creator who openly documents AI assistance is more credible than one who appears to hide it.
3. **Accuracy:** Claiming purely human authorship for AI-assisted work creates legal vulnerability if discovered.

## AI CONTRIBUTION DISCLOSURE RECORD

```yaml
# AI GENERATION DISCLOSURE RECORD
# Project ID: YDM-[ID]

ai_disclosure:
  
  ai_used: "Yes | No"
  
  ai_tools_used:
    - tool_name: "[AI MODEL/TOOL NAME — e.g., Midjourney v6, GPT-4, Stable Diffusion]"
      tool_type: "Image generation | Text generation | Code generation | Audio | Video | 3D | Other"
      usage_description: "[WHAT WAS GENERATED WITH THIS TOOL]"
      
  human_contribution_documentation:
    creative_direction: "[HOW THE HUMAN DIRECTED THE AI — prompts, parameters, style guidance]"
    selection_and_curation: "[HOW THE HUMAN SELECTED FROM AI OUTPUTS]"
    modification_and_editing: "[HOW THE HUMAN MODIFIED AI OUTPUTS]"
    integration_with_human_work: "[HOW AI OUTPUTS WERE COMBINED WITH HUMAN-MADE ELEMENTS]"
    
  contribution_proportion:
    human_created_elements: "[WHAT PERCENTAGE/WHICH ELEMENTS ARE PURELY HUMAN-MADE]"
    ai_generated_elements: "[WHAT PERCENTAGE/WHICH ELEMENTS WERE AI-GENERATED]"
    hybrid_elements: "[WHAT WAS BOTH AI-GENERATED AND HUMAN-MODIFIED]"
    
  legal_status_note: >
    The copyright status of AI-generated content varies significantly
    by jurisdiction. As of 2024, purely AI-generated content without
    meaningful human authorship may not be eligible for copyright
    protection in many jurisdictions. This record documents the human
    creative contribution that establishes the basis for any rights claim.
    Consult a qualified IP attorney for jurisdiction-specific guidance.
    
  disclosure_level:
    public_disclosure: "Full | Partial | None — REASON: [REASON IF NONE]"
    disclosure_format: "[HOW AI USE IS DISCLOSED — e.g., in credits, metadata, documentation]"
```

## HUMAN CONTRIBUTION EVIDENCE

The most important documentation for AI-assisted works is evidence of human creative direction:

| Evidence Type | Description | Strength |
|--------------|-------------|---------|
| Saved prompts | The specific creative direction given to AI | Strong |
| Iteration records | Multiple AI outputs showing selection process | Strong |
| Human edits | Before/after showing human modification of AI output | Very strong |
| Concept sketches | Human sketches that preceded AI generation | Very strong |
| Brief/direction document | Written creative brief that guided AI use | Strong |
