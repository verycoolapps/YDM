## DISCIPLINE OVERVIEW

Business methodologies occupy complex IP territory. The specific expression of a framework (diagrams, documents, branded terminology) can be protected. The underlying method or process may require patent protection if truly novel.

---

## SPECIALIZED PROTECTION CHECKLIST

```
BUSINESS FRAMEWORK PROTECTION CHECKLIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT CAN BE PROTECTED:
✅ The specific diagram layouts and visual presentations
✅ The original written explanations and descriptions
✅ Branded terminology (can be trademarked)
✅ The specific combination and expression of concepts
✅ Training materials and documentation

WHAT TYPICALLY CANNOT BE PROTECTED:
❌ The underlying business method itself (without patent)
❌ Generic business concepts that pre-exist your framework
❌ Common management principles

PROTECTION STEPS:
□ Document the original framework in writing with a clear date
□ Preserve the first presentation/whitepaper/document
□ Register distinctive branded names as trademarks
□ Copyright notice on all published materials
□ For genuinely novel processes: consult patent attorney
□ Trade secret protection for unpublished elements (maintain secrecy)

□ YDM provenance record established
```
