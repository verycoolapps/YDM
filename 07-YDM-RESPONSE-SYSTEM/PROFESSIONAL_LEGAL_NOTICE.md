## PROFESSIONAL CORRESPONDENCE TYPES

Beyond the signature YOUR DEAD MEAT Notice, the response system supports:

### Type A — Platform Rights Report
Used to report infringing content to a platform (not the infringer directly).

**Key elements:**
- Clear identification of original work and rights holder
- Specific URL(s) of infringing content
- Statement of rights basis
- Formal takedown request
- Good faith statement
- Personal attestation of accuracy

→ See `PLATFORM_TAKEDOWN_PROTOCOL.md` for platform-specific guidance

### Type B — Professional Inquiry
An initial, non-aggressive inquiry seeking explanation before making accusations.

```
PROFESSIONAL INQUIRY TEMPLATE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Subject: Inquiry Regarding [WORK/PLATFORM/MATERIAL] — YDM-[ID]

Dear [NAME/PLATFORM],

I am [NAME], the creator of [WORK]. I have recently noticed
[MATERIAL] at [LOCATION] that appears to share significant
similarities with my original work.

Before pursuing any formal process, I would like to understand
the origin of this material. Could you please let me know:

1. When and how [THE MATERIAL] was created?
2. Whether you are aware of [ORIGINAL WORK]?
3. Whether there is any license or authorization I should be
   aware of?

I look forward to your response within [14 DAYS].

Sincerely,
[NAME]
```

### Type C — Post-Response Escalation
Used when the recipient has responded but their response is inadequate.

### Type D — Settlement/Resolution Offer
When seeking a negotiated resolution rather than formal enforcement.
