## C&D GENERATION PRINCIPLES

A cease and desist letter accomplishes these goals when done correctly:

1. **Documents your position** — creates a record that you asserted your rights
2. **Demands specific action** — clear, achievable requests
3. **Sets a reasonable timeline** — not arbitrary, not impossibly short
4. **Reserves legal rights** — without committing to litigation prematurely
5. **Establishes professionalism** — demonstrates you are serious and organized

A C&D fails when it:
- Contains unlawful threats
- Makes claims not supported by evidence
- Demands impossible things
- Uses abusive or personal language
- Claims legal authority it doesn't have

## BEFORE GENERATING A C&D

Required conditions:

```
PRE-C&D CHECKLIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ Case investigation completed (Mode C)
□ Evidence quality: Moderate or Strong (not Insufficient)
□ Infringement assessment completed
□ Jurisdiction identified
□ All evidence preserved and organized
□ Legal counsel review recommended? → YES → Flag for attorney review

If evidence quality is LOW or INSUFFICIENT:
  → Do not generate a formal C&D
  → Generate internal documentation only
  → Collect additional evidence before proceeding
```
