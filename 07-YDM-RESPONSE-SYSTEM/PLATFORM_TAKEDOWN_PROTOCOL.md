## PLATFORM TAKEDOWN GUIDE

### Understanding Platform Takedowns

Most major platforms have intellectual property reporting mechanisms. Using these is often faster and more practical than legal proceedings.

**Important:** Filing a false DMCA takedown (US) or equivalent in other jurisdictions can result in liability. File only when you have a genuine, good-faith belief that infringement has occurred.

### DMCA Takedown Notice Elements (US)

A valid DMCA Notice under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) must include:

```
DMCA TAKEDOWN NOTICE — REQUIRED ELEMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. IDENTIFICATION OF COPYRIGHTED WORK:
   [Describe the original work and where it can be found]

2. IDENTIFICATION OF INFRINGING MATERIAL:
   [Specific URL(s) of the infringing material]

3. CONTACT INFORMATION:
   [Name, address, phone, email of complaining party]

4. GOOD FAITH STATEMENT:
   "I have a good faith belief that use of the material in
   the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright
   owner, its agent, or the law."

5. ACCURACY STATEMENT:
   "The information in this notification is accurate and,
   under penalty of perjury, I am the copyright owner or am
   authorized to act on behalf of the owner."

6. SIGNATURE:
   [Electronic or physical signature]
```

### Pre-Takedown Checklist

```
BEFORE FILING A TAKEDOWN:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ Evidence quality assessed as Moderate or Strong
□ False positive checks completed
□ You own (or represent) the rights being claimed
□ You have a good faith belief infringement has occurred
□ You understand that false DMCA claims carry legal risk
□ You have legal counsel review recommended (optional for clear cases,
  strongly recommended for complex situations)
```
