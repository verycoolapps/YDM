## RESOLUTION STRATEGIES

IP disputes don't always require litigation. Professional resolution is usually faster, cheaper, and preserves working relationships.

### Resolution Options (Least to Most Formal)

| Level | Approach | When Appropriate |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Direct dialogue | Good-faith infringer, minor incident |
| 2 | Formal inquiry | Unclear intent, need explanation |
| 3 | YOUR DEAD MEAT Notice | Established evidence, want formal response |
| 4 | Platform takedown | Content on a platform, want removal |
| 5 | Negotiated settlement | Both parties want resolution without court |
| 6 | Mediation | Parties willing to compromise, need neutral |
| 7 | Arbitration | Parties agreed in contract to arbitrate |
| 8 | Litigation | Last resort; evidence is strong; legal counsel required |

### Settlement Considerations

What can be negotiated:
- **Attribution correction** — proper credit going forward
- **License retroactive** — legitimize past use with payment/agreement
- **Removal agreement** — work removed from specific platforms
- **Profit sharing** — share of commercial gains from infringing work
- **Non-disparagement** — no public statements about the dispute
- **Non-repetition** — commitment to not repeat the infringement

**Always have settlement agreements reviewed by qualified legal counsel before signing.**
