## ESCALATION DECISION MATRIX

This matrix determines the appropriate response level based on evidence quality and case severity.

```
ESCALATION MATRIX
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LEVEL 0 — DOCUMENT ONLY
Evidence: Any level
Action: Internal documentation; no external action
Trigger: Insufficient evidence OR low impact

LEVEL 1 — INFORMAL INQUIRY
Evidence: Low to Moderate
Trigger: Noticeable similarity; want explanation before escalating
Action: Professional Inquiry letter (non-accusatory)
Legal counsel: Optional

LEVEL 2 — YOUR DEAD MEAT NOTICE
Evidence: Moderate to Strong
Trigger: Clear evidence of copying; formal response needed
Action: YOUR_DEAD_MEAT_NOTICE.md — formal demand
Legal counsel: Strongly recommended before sending

LEVEL 3 — PLATFORM TAKEDOWN
Evidence: Moderate to Strong
Trigger: Content on a platform; faster removal needed
Action: PLATFORM_TAKEDOWN_PROTOCOL.md — formal report
Legal counsel: Recommended (required for complex DMCA situations)

LEVEL 4 — LEGAL COUNSEL + FORMAL ACTION
Evidence: Strong
Trigger: High value; failed prior notices; ongoing infringement
Action: Engage qualified IP attorney
Legal counsel: REQUIRED

LEVEL 5 — LITIGATION (LAST RESORT)
Evidence: Very Strong
Trigger: All prior levels failed; significant ongoing harm
Action: Through qualified legal counsel only
Legal counsel: ESSENTIAL
```

### Escalation Decision Tool

```
ESCALATION DECISION — STEP BY STEP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEP 1: What is the evidence quality?
  Insufficient → Level 0 only
  Low → Level 0 or 1
  Moderate → Level 1 or 2 depending on impact
  Strong → Level 2, 3, or 4 depending on impact and history

STEP 2: What is the impact?
  Low impact → Consider lower escalation levels
  Moderate impact → Standard escalation levels
  High impact (commercial, wide distribution) → Consider Level 3-4 immediately

STEP 3: Has prior communication failed?
  No prior communication → Start at appropriate level for evidence
  Prior informal contact ignored → Escalate one level
  Prior formal notice ignored → Escalate one-two levels

STEP 4: Is legal counsel involved?
  No → Strongly consider involving counsel at Level 2+
  Yes → Proceed with counsel's guidance
```
