## PATENT CONSIDERATIONS

**Important:** YDM is primarily a copyright and trademark documentation tool. Patent protection is more complex and expensive, and always requires a qualified patent attorney.

### When Patents May Be Relevant

- Novel software algorithms (jurisdictions vary significantly on software patent eligibility)
- New functional product designs
- Novel manufacturing processes
- Unique mechanical innovations

### Patent Types

| Type | What It Covers | Duration |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Utility Patent | Function and process | ~20 years from filing |
| Design Patent | Ornamental appearance of an article | ~15 years (US) |
| Plant Patent | New variety of asexually reproduced plant | ~20 years |
| Provisional Patent (US) | Establishes priority date without full examination | 12 months (then must file utility) |

### Patent vs. Copyright for Software

In many jurisdictions:
- **Code as text:** Copyright protects the specific code expression
- **Algorithm/process:** May be patentable in some jurisdictions (not others)
- **UI design:** May be protectable as design patent and/or copyright
- **API:** Copyright protection is debated; patent may apply to underlying process

**Key Principle:** Copyright and patent can protect different aspects of the same work.

### Patent Awareness Flags

YDM will flag for patent consideration when:
- Project involves a genuinely novel technical process
- Project involves a unique mechanical or functional design
- User explicitly asks about protecting an invention

**Always redirect to qualified patent attorney for patent matters.**
