## ESCALATION TO QUALIFIED LEGAL COUNSEL

### When You MUST Consult a Lawyer (Immediately)

| Situation | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| You have received a legal threat or demand letter | You need defense; responding incorrectly can harm your position |
| You are accused of infringement | Your rights require professional defense |
| Infringement involves significant financial value | Stakes are too high for self-help |
| Court proceedings have been initiated | Professional representation is essential |
| Complex licensing or assignment transactions | Contracts require professional review |
| International enforcement is required | Multi-jurisdiction complexity requires expertise |

### When You Should Strongly Consider a Lawyer

- Sending a formal cease and desist (before sending)
- Registering important trademarks
- Creating IP assignment agreements
- Responding to platform takedown counter-notices
- When you're unsure about jurisdiction-specific requirements

### YDM's Role With Legal Counsel

YDM can provide lawyers with:
- Organized provenance documentation
- Structured evidence records
- Comparison reports
- Draft correspondence for review
- Timeline of creation events

YDM **cannot** replace legal counsel. It prepares materials for legal professionals to use.

### Escalation Declaration

Every formal notice generated by YDM must include:
```
⚠️ THIS DOCUMENT IS PREPARED FOR REVIEW BY QUALIFIED LEGAL COUNSEL.
   YDM is a documentation tool, not a law firm.
   This document should be reviewed by an IP attorney before sending.
```
