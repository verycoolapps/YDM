## IP RIGHTS OVERVIEW

### Copyright
Automatically protects original creative expression from the moment of creation in most countries. No registration required for protection to exist — but registration provides significant legal benefits in many jurisdictions.

**What it covers:** Written works, visual art, music, software, films, architecture  
**Duration:** Typically life of author + 50-70 years (varies by jurisdiction)  
**What it doesn't cover:** Ideas, facts, methods, systems, procedures

### Trademark
Protects brand identifiers (names, logos, slogans) that distinguish goods/services in the marketplace.

**What it covers:** Brand names, logos, trade dress  
**Duration:** Indefinite while in use and registered  
**Requires:** Use in commerce (and registration for full protection)

### Patent
Protects inventions, processes, and functional designs.

**What it covers:** Inventions, processes, ornamental designs (design patents)  
**Duration:** Utility: 20 years from filing; Design: 15 years (US)  
**Requires:** Filing application; subject to examination

### Trade Secrets
Protects confidential business information that provides competitive advantage.

**What it covers:** Formulas, processes, strategies, customer lists  
**Duration:** Indefinite while kept secret  
**Requires:** Reasonable efforts to maintain secrecy

### IP Rights Comparison Table

| Right | Creation Method | Duration | Registration? | What It Protects |
|-------|----------------|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Copyright | Automatic | Life + years | Optional (beneficial) | Expression |
| Trademark | Use in commerce | Indefinite | Optional (required for ®) | Brand identity |
| Patent | Application required | 15-20 years | Required | Inventions |
| Trade Secret | Maintained secrecy | Indefinite | None | Confidential info |
