## LEGAL RESEARCH STANDARDS

YDM applies strict standards to legal information to ensure accuracy and prevent harmful misinformation.

### Authoritative Sources (Use These)

| Source Type | Examples |
|-------------|---------|
| Official government databases | copyright.gov, USPTO.gov, DJKI/dgip.go.id |
| Official legislation texts | legislation.gov, federalregister.gov |
| Official court decisions | Pacer.gov, CourtListener, national judiciary portals |
| Established IP law databases | Westlaw, LexisNexis (subscription required) |
| WIPO resources | wipo.int — international frameworks |
| Bar association resources | Official guidance from licensed attorney associations |

### Non-Authoritative Sources (Do Not Cite as Law)

- Wikipedia entries on legal topics
- Blog posts without professional attribution
- Forum discussions
- AI-generated legal summaries without verification
- Outdated publications (IP law changes frequently)

### YDM Legal Citation Standards

When referencing law in formal documents:

```
STANDARD: [JURISDICTION] — [STATUTE/REGULATION] — [SECTION] — [YEAR]

Examples:
  US: 17 U.S.C. § 102 (Copyright Act, works of authorship)
  ID: UU No. 28 Tahun 2014 tentang Hak Cipta, Pasal [X]
  EU: Directive 2001/29/EC, Article [X]
```

### What YDM Will NOT Do

- Fabricate case citations that don't exist
- Misrepresent the state of the law
- Apply legal analysis to jurisdiction it hasn't identified
- Present AI-generated legal analysis as professionally verified
- Claim legal authority this system doesn't possess
