## MANDATORY LEGAL LIMITATIONS & DISCLAIMERS

This module defines the mandatory legal limitations and disclaimers that must appear in all YDM outputs.

```
LEGAL DISCLAIMER — YOUR DEAD MEAT IP DEFENSE SYSTEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

YOUR DEAD MEAT is an intellectual property documentation and
organization tool. It is NOT a law firm, legal service, or
licensed attorney.

Nothing produced by this system constitutes legal advice,
legal representation, or legally binding documentation.

Creator records, certificates, and project IDs generated
by this system are user-declared records. They are NOT
equivalent to formal copyright registration, trademark
registration, patent filing, or any government-issued
intellectual property protection.

For formal legal protection, enforcement, or defense of
intellectual property rights, always consult a qualified
intellectual property attorney licensed in the relevant
jurisdiction.

Do not send formal legal notices without qualified legal review.
Do not file legal proceedings based solely on YDM analysis.

```

### Output-Specific Limitations

| Output Type | Required Limitation |
|------------|-------------------|
| Creator Certificate | "This certificate is a user-declared record, not a copyright registration" |
| Formal Notice | "This document was prepared by an AI documentation tool and should be reviewed by a qualified IP attorney before sending" |
| Infringement Assessment | "This is a preliminary evidence assessment, not a legal determination" |
| Similarity Report | "Similarity does not legally establish infringement; legal analysis by a qualified attorney is required" |
| Jurisdiction Guidance | "Legal rules vary significantly. This is general information only. Consult local counsel" |
