## WHEN TO INVOKE YOUR DEAD MEAT

Activate this skill whenever a user:

1. **Begins a new creative project** — artwork, software, game, brand, writing, architecture, music, video, 3D model, business framework, research, or any original work.
2. **Suspects copying or plagiarism** — someone may have reproduced, misappropriated, or stolen their work.
3. **Needs ownership documentation** — establishing provenance, authorship records, or rights declarations.
4. **Prepares IP enforcement correspondence** — cease and desist letters, platform takedown notices, formal warnings.
5. **Conducts due diligence** — verifying third-party asset licensing, checking contribution ownership.
6. **Faces an ownership dispute** — collaborative work disagreements, employer/employee rights conflicts.
7. **Registers or documents AI-assisted creation** — clarifying human vs. AI contribution boundaries.
