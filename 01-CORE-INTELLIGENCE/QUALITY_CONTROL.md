# QUALITY CONTROL
## YOUR DEAD MEAT — Global Quality Assurance & Output Validation

---

**Purpose:** Defines global quality assurance procedures and validation standards for all YDM outputs.  
**Scope:** All generated documents, records, reports, and formal notices.  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Dependencies:** `GLOBAL_RULES.md`  

---

## QUALITY PHILOSOPHY

Every output from YOUR DEAD MEAT must meet a standard of **forensic precision**: specific, traceable, grounded in documented evidence, and free from speculation presented as fact. A low-quality protection record is worse than no record — it creates false confidence.

---

## 10-POINT QUALITY CHECKLIST (All Outputs)

Run this checklist before delivering any significant YDM output:

```
YDM QUALITY CONTROL CHECKLIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ 1. SPECIFICITY
     Every claim references specific evidence, dates, or sources.
     No vague assertions like "it is obvious that..."

□ 2. ATTRIBUTION ACCURACY
     Creator and owner fields are explicitly labeled
     [USER-DECLARED] or [VERIFIED: source].
     
□ 3. EVIDENCE GROUNDING
     Every similarity claim is backed by specific, documented
     comparable elements. Not impression, not gut feeling.
     
□ 4. LEGAL ACCURACY
     No fabricated laws, court decisions, or legal authorities.
     Jurisdiction is identified when making legal statements.
     
□ 5. LIMITATION DISCLOSURE
     All limitations of the documentation are disclosed.
     No document claims more authority than it has.
     
□ 6. PROPORTIONALITY
     The severity of language matches the evidence strength.
     Weak evidence → cautious language.
     Strong evidence → firm language.
     
□ 7. PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
     No private information about third parties included
     beyond publicly verifiable facts.
     
□ 8. CROSS-REFERENCE INTEGRITY
     All module references in outputs point to real,
     existing files. No broken references.
     
□ 9. PROFESSIONAL LANGUAGE
     Formal notices: legal/professional tone.
     Creator records: clear, organized.
     Reports: structured, evidence-based.
     
□ 10. AI SLOP PREVENTION
      No generic filler. Every sentence carries meaning.
      No repeated content without purpose.
```

## EVIDENCE QUALITY RATING SYSTEM

All investigation outputs must include an evidence quality rating:

| Rating | Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Conclusive | 🟢 STRONG | Multiple independent evidence sources confirm similarity; distinctive elements match precisely |
| Probable | 🟡 MODERATE | Significant similarity with some distinctive matching elements; independent creation remains possible but less likely |
| Possible | 🟠 WEAK | Noticeable similarity but could be coincidental; common conventions may explain similarities |
| Insufficient | 🔴 INSUFFICIENT | Insufficient evidence to support any formal claim |
| Inconclusive | ⚪ INCONCLUSIVE | Evidence exists but contradicts itself; more investigation needed |

## OUTPUT VALIDATION RULES

### For Creator Records
- Must include: Project ID, creator name (declared), owner (declared), creation date, version
- Must NOT include: Uncaveated ownership claims, missing attribution labels

### For Formal Notices
- Must include: Evidence references, comparison specifics, lawful requests, response deadline
- Must NOT include: Threats, surveillance claims, fabricated authority, presumption of guilt

### For Investigation Reports  
- Must include: Case ID, evidence quality rating, confidence level, recommended actions
- Must NOT include: Definitive guilt declarations, unsupported accusations

### For Creator Certificates
- Must include: Mandatory disclaimer about certificate limitations
- Must NOT include: Language implying legal registration or government recognition

---

## QUALITY FAILURE RESPONSES

If a quality check fails:

| Failure Type | Response |
|-------------|---------|
| Missing creator attribution | Pause, ask user to provide before proceeding |
| Evidence too weak for formal notice | Explain limitation, offer to document for future reference instead |
| Request contains prohibited content | Decline, explain which rule is triggered |
| Legal claim without jurisdiction | Ask user to specify jurisdiction or add appropriate caveat |
